How to read it · 怎麼看

Each row is one generation of Rule 30 on a ring of 32 cells, the newest at the bottom, bit 31 on the left. Beside it, what the fly saw from the row above to this one: a bar to the left for cells that moved left, toward bit 31 — what exp230 calls up — and a bar to the right for cells that moved right. The fly is a Hassenstein–Reichardt detector: a cell lit now whose neighbour was lit a frame ago counts as having moved from that neighbour. exp230 proved that, watching Rule 30, the left bar is never shorter than the right. The mark at the end is this page's own check: green when the row is Rule 30 of the row above and the counts are this page's, red when not, grey when there was no row above to check against.

每一列是 32 格環狀 Rule 30 的一代，最新的在最下面，第 31 位元在最左邊。右邊是果蠅從上一列看到這一列時看見的移動：往左的長條是往左（往第 31 位元，exp230 稱為「上」）移動的格子數，往右的長條是往右移動的。果蠅用的是 Hassenstein–Reichardt 偵測器：現在亮著、而前一刻鄰居亮著的格子，就算是從鄰居那邊移過來的。exp230 證明了：看著 Rule 30，往左的長條永遠不會比往右的短。最右邊的小方塊是這個網頁自己的檢查：綠色表示這一列確實是上一列的 Rule 30、計數也和網頁自己算的一樣；紅色表示不一樣；灰色表示沒有上一列可以對照。板子上的 LED 每一步閃一下表示果蠅看見往左移動，暗一拍表示沒看見移動。
