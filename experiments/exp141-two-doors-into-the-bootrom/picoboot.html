Can a browser drive the bootrom's flash interface?

exp141 · the board must be in BOOTSEL mode (2e8a:000f). This page only reads — it claims PICOBOOT and asks its status. It writes nothing to flash.

Put the board in BOOTSEL, then press Connect.

(the PICOBOOT interface and its reply will appear here)
What this proves, and what it does not. BOOTSEL exposes two USB interfaces: a mass-storage drive (the one you drag .uf2 onto) and a vendor interface called PICOBOOT that picotool drives. Chrome's WebUSB refuses to claim mass storage, and — as this repository found the hard way — Android's storage layer writes to that drive unreliably. This page claims the other door, PICOBOOT, and confirms a browser can talk to it. It stops before writing anything: flashing is the next experiment.
Nothing here can brick the board. It sends two control requests — reset the interface, read the status — and no flash command. Even if it did, BOOTSEL is the recovery state, not a state you fall out of.
