Erase a stuck board's flash, over PICOBOOT

exp141 · for a board whose flash carries a partition table (exp139) that made drag-and-drop flashing refuse ordinary UF2s. The board must be in BOOTSEL (2e8a:000f). No app, no udev, no toolchain — Chrome on Android or desktop.

Put the board in BOOTSEL, then press Erase.

This one writes. Unlike this experiment's read-only picoboot.html, it erases the first 64 KB of flash — the partition table and anything after it. That is exactly what un-sticks a board exp139 bricked, and it is harmless to a board that is already stuck: BOOTSEL is the recovery state, and an erased board just sits in it waiting for a UF2.
After it succeeds: the board is a blank, stock board again. Drag any ordinary .uf2 onto its RP2350 drive — exp103 to see the LED blink, for instance — and it flashes normally, because the table that was in the way is gone.
