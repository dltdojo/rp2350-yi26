What this page is for. It exists to answer one question nobody has answered: with a log page holding the CDC interface in another tab, does a draw sent from here still reach it? If it does, two channels give back the composability that merging the log into one page took away. If the other tab is asleep and misses the line, merging was right.

It also tests something simpler and equally unverified — whether a page can claim a vendor interface at all. Nothing in this repository had tried.
